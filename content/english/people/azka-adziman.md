Azka is a EPSRC Summer Research Intern (2026) at the Mobile Robotics Group at the University of Oxford working with Daniele De Martini, Perla Maiolino and Marco Pontin (PDRA) of the Soft Robotics Lab on a deformable TOF sensor array.

His internship focuses on developing an reconfigurable origami/folding time-of-flight sensor array for research into exploring active sensing methods.

He is currently pursuing an MEng in Engineering Science at Oxford, specialising in Machine Learning, Information Engineering and Control Theory.
