Siqi is a DPhil student in the MRG and the Dynamic Robot Systems Group, supervised by Prof. Daniele De Martini and Prof. Maurice Fallon. His research interests lie in the field of computer vision and robotics, such as visual localization, 3D reconstruction, and robotic perception. He hopes his work will enable spatial intelligence in robots, allowing them to better understand and interact with the world, and contribute to next-generation technologies in AR/VR, autonomous driving, and embodied AI. Previously, he completed his Master’s degree at New York University, where he worked with Prof. Chen Feng in the AI4CE Lab.
