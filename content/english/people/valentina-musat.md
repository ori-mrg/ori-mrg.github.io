Valentina joined ORI in 2020 as a DPhil, under the supervision of Prof. Paul Newman. She completed a BSc in Computer Science at Oxford Brookes University and previously, an MSc in Economics and Finance at Copenhagen Business School. 
Her current research interests are related to data generation for downstream tasks and in her free time she works with the Oxford Brookes Racing Autonomous team.
