I am an Assistant Professor of Robotics and AI in the Department of Computer and Software Engineering at Polytechnique Montréal (Université de Montréal).

In 2024, I was a Visiting PhD Student at the University of Oxford, where I worked with the Mobile Robotics Group under the supervision of Dr. Daniele De Martini.

My research interests focus on robust, efficient, and adaptive robotic perception for single- and multi-agent systems.
