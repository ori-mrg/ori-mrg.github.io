summary: >-
  The Oxford Offroad Radar Dataset contains radar recordings and GPS/INS reference
  measurements collected in the Scottish Highlands, including extreme weather. Eleven
  traversals cover approximately 154 km across four routes with distinct landscapes and
  driving conditions. An accompanying toolkit supports radar place-recognition
  experiments and evaluation against reference positions.
