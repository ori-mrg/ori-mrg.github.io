# Summary source: https://oxford-robotics-institute.github.io/radar-robotcar-dataset/
summary: >-
  This extension to the Oxford RobotCar Dataset adds scanning millimetre-wave radar and
  dual 3D lidar recordings from 280 km of driving around Oxford. Optimised radar
  odometry ground truth accompanies the sensor data. It supports research into
  radar-based localisation, mapping, and odometry, including conditions that are
  challenging for cameras and other sensors.
