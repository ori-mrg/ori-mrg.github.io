# Summary source: https://robotcar-dataset.robots.ox.ac.uk/
summary: >-
  The Oxford RobotCar Dataset records more than 100 traversals of a repeated route
  through central Oxford over more than a year. It combines camera imagery, lidar, and
  GPS/INS measurements across changing weather, lighting, traffic, and roadworks. These
  repeated observations support research into long-term localisation and mapping in
  changing urban environments.
