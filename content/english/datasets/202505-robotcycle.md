summary: >-
  RobotCycle captures more than 70 km of urban cycling from the cyclist’s viewpoint,
  combining cameras, lidar, inertial sensing, and eye-gaze tracking. The recordings span
  varied road layouts, traffic, weather, terrain, and inclines. An accompanying toolkit
  supports perception, odometry, and analysis of how cyclist attention and behaviour
  relate to road infrastructure and safety.
