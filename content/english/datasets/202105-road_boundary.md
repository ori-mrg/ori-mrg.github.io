# Summary source: https://oxford-robotics-institute.github.io/road-boundaries-dataset/
summary: >-
  The Oxford Road Boundaries Dataset pairs urban driving images with road-boundary masks
  for training and evaluating detection methods. Manual annotations from two RobotCar
  traversals are extended to additional journeys using visual localisation, producing
  62,605 labelled samples, including 47,639 curated samples. The data covers varied road
  layouts, traffic, weather, and lighting conditions.
