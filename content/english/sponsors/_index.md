---

### How We Collaborate

We offer flexible partnership models tailored to your organisation’s goals:

- **Industry-Funded Research Projects**: Targeted research and development projects with agreed deliverables such as algorithms, datasets, or prototype systems.
- **Sponsored Studentships**: Co-designed PhD or postdoctoral research projects with shared supervision, defined milestones, and regular progress reporting.
- **Internships & Placements**: Host MRG students for internships or placements to explore practical applications of research within your organisation.
- **Consultancy & Secondments**: Short-term embedded collaborations to address specific technical challenges.
- **Financial & In-kind Support**: Direct financial contributions or in-kind support — such as sensors, robotic platforms, datasets, or compute infrastructure — that directly enable and accelerate our research.

Custom arrangements are available — we are happy to discuss a partnership model that aligns with your strategic objectives.
